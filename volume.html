The Vector Discipline

Adobe Illustrator

Illustrator is the industry standard for vector graphics — logos, icons, packaging, type systems, and technical illustration. This volume builds your command of the application from first launch through professional-grade path construction. Every shape, every letterform, every logo you will ever produce in Illustrator is made of paths. This volume teaches you to see them, build them, and control them with precision.
