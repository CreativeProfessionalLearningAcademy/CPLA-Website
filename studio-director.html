Lesson Brief

Module 1 — What Design Is

Volume 1 · Lessons 1.1–1.3 · Foundations of Design Thinking

Objectives

  • Students can define design as intentional problem-solving, not decoration.
  • Students can identify design in everyday objects and communication.
  • Students can describe the basic stages of the design process.

Talking Points

  • "Design is how it works, not just how it looks."
  • Every designed thing solves a problem for a person.
  • Professionals design on purpose — with a brief, an audience, and constraints.

Common Misconceptions

  • "Design = making things pretty." (It's problem-solving first.)
  • "Good designers just have talent." (It's a repeatable process.)
  • "There's one right answer." (Design weighs trade-offs.)

Demonstrate & Discuss

  • Show two versions of an everyday object; ask which "works" better and why.
  • Walk the design process: understand → ideate → make → refine.
  • Discussion: "What's something you use daily that's well designed? Poorly?"
